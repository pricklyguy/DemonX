## How to agree

Add this sentence to your first pull request (or to a comment on it):

> I have read the DemonX Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md) and I agree to it for this and all my future
> contributions to DemonX.

If You contribute on behalf of an organisation, say so in the sentence and name the organisation, and make sure You
are allowed to agree for it.

PGC keeps a record of the agreement (the pull request). You only need to agree once.
